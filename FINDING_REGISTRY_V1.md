# HealthOS Finding Registry V1

V1 intentionally starts with ten reproducible findings: sustained HRV drop, sustained RHR elevation, recovery concordance, sleep deficit, acute training-load increase, recovery/load mismatch, weight trend, SpO2 deviation, insufficient recent data and source discontinuity.

Every finding records inputs, period, observed/reference values when applicable, effect size, robust z when defined, sample count, coverage, confounders, evidence strength, detector version and an interpretation boundary.

`evidence_strength` is categorical (INSUFFICIENT/LOW/MODERATE/HIGH), not an uncalibrated pseudo-probability. Findings describe reproducible longitudinal changes; they do not manufacture causality or diagnosis.
