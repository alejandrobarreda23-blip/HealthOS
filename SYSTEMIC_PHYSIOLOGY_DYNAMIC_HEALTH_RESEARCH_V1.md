# HealthOS Systemic Physiology & Dynamic Health — Research V1

## Status
Research architecture. Not a production score and not a medical claim.

## Core frame
HealthOS studies how the organism maintains function, responds to perturbations, recovers equilibrium and adapts over time.

The unit of observation can be the datum. The unit of learning should progressively become the response.

Current analytical chain:
`metric → baseline → deviation → finding`

Research extension:
`state → perturbation → multisystem response → recovery → adaptation`

## Four dimensions
1. **State** — how are systems now?
2. **Trajectory** — how are systems changing?
3. **Dynamics** — how does the organism respond and recover?
4. **Adaptive capacity** — is response to comparable perturbations changing over time?

## Dynamic Aging
Keep two jurisdictions separate:
- Structural/State Aging: the existing seven-system trajectories.
- Dynamic/Adaptive Aging: response amplitude, recovery time/completeness, functional reserve, adaptive gain, cross-system recovery sequence and resilience trajectory.

Dynamic Aging is not a biological-age calculator and must not be merged into HealthOS Pace without calibration and validation.

## Constitutional rules
- SYS-01 Health is not inferred from isolated variables alone.
- SYS-02 Responses require context.
- SYS-03 Recovery and adaptation are first-class longitudinal information.
- SYS-04 Missing is not physiology.
- SYS-05 Perturbation is not valence.
- SYS-06 Response magnitude is not automatically pathology.
- SYS-07 Cross-system interaction is not automatically health.
- SYS-08 Systemic constructs require explicit validation.
- SYS-09 Structural Aging and Dynamic Aging remain distinct.
- SYS-10 Personal and population evidence remain separate jurisdictions.
- SYS-11 AI does not compute authoritative systemic physiology.
- SYS-12 New systemic layers must be re-derivable from raw/normalized evidence plus algorithm versions.

## Research lines

## Explicit non-goals
Do not create a Systemic Health Score, wearable-proxy Allostatic Load Score, Network Health Score, Resilience Age, Dynamic Biological Age, causal graph from correlations, or microbiome “master system” without independent validation.
