# Scientific Intelligence V1.8

Pipeline: SOURCE → CLAIMS → APPRAISAL → CORROBORATION/CONFLICT → MODEL UPDATE GATE → HUMAN REVIEW → VERSIONED ADOPTION.

A paper can create a model-change proposal or a measurement candidate. It cannot silently edit production references, evidence weights or decision rules.

Appraisal separates internal validity, directness, precision, consistency, applicability and outcome importance.

Measurement Intelligence separates **information gain** from **clinical utility** and penalizes burden, invasiveness and radiation.

The REACT example is intentionally represented as emerging measurement evidence: direct plaque imaging may reveal subclinical disease missed by indirect risk estimation, while benefit from systematic early screening remains unresolved. Therefore Health OS may flag vascular imaging as an informative candidate, but does not automatically recommend screening or mutate the cardiovascular model.

Future Literature Watch should ingest authoritative PubMed/Crossref/guideline feeds with DOI/PMID, fingerprints and source spans; LLMs may extract/explain, while deterministic gates control model mutation.
