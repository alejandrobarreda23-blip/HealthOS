# Event Semantics V0.9

An event is not an interpretation.

`sauna` is converted into explicit temporal exposures. Those exposures may be tested against candidate outcomes defined by the event registry.

Knowledge ladder:

1. Event — reported fact
2. Exposure — deterministic temporal derivation
3. Association — statistical relationship
4. Hypothesis — explicit, versioned interpretation
5. Experiment — future N-of-1 intervention
6. Personal conclusion — only after adequate evidence

## Guardrails
- No event automatically creates a health conclusion.
- Candidate outcomes mean “worth testing”, not “expected effect”.
- Confounders are explicit and event-specific.
- Associations retain sample sizes, method, adjustment variables and evidence.
- Low sample counts return `insufficient`.
- LLMs may explain an association but may not manufacture one.
