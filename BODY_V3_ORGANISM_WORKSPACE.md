# HealthOS Body V3 — Organism Workspace

## Purpose

Body is the navigable physiological index of HealthOS. It is not a clinical traffic light and it does not diagnose from anatomy.

## Visual constitution

- Color testifies; it does not judge.
- Movement requires an eligible `measured` channel.
- Derived, reported and inferred evidence remain visually distinct and static by default.
- Fog means missing/insufficient observability, never zero or normality.
- Amber is reserved for an active, traceable finding.
- Historical gaps remain gaps.
- Historical observations may be reconstructed from normalized records; historical findings are not silently recomputed or projected backwards.

## Body V3 additions

- Modular system cards and physiological figure.
- System-specific channels with local missingness fog.
- Exact-day 365-day scrubber over normalized observations and exercise sessions.
- Explicit evidence/provenance per selected system.
- Current-vs-history distinction.
- Current deterministic findings are mapped to systems only when the finding is physiological; data-quality findings do not become anatomical beacons.
- Motion is gated by normalized evidence marked `measured`.

## Systems in V3

- Autonomic
- Cardiorespiratory
- Sleep
- Musculoskeletal
- Metabolic
- Recovery

This is a navigation grammar, not a claim that these six visual systems are a validated complete ontology of human physiology.
